# CS 4910 Project Presentation Template

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- Project Title
- Team Members' Names
- Course: CS 4910 - Introduction to Computer Security
- Date

## Slide 2: Project Overview
- Brief description of the project
- Problem statement or research question
- Objectives and goals

## Slide 3: Background & Motivation
- Why this topic is important
- Current state of the field
- Relevance to cybersecurity

## Slide 4: Related Work
- Brief overview of existing solutions or research
- Gaps or limitations in current approaches
- How your project addresses these gaps

## Slide 5: Methodology
- Approach taken
- Tools and technologies used
- Implementation environment

## Slide 6: System Architecture/Design
- High-level design of your solution
- Components and their interactions
- Security considerations in the design

## Slides 7-8: Implementation Details
- Technical details of your implementation
- Key features or components
- Challenges encountered and how they were addressed

## Slides 9-10: Demonstration
- Screenshots or diagrams of your implementation
- Key functionality
- Security features implemented

## Slide 11: Results & Analysis
- Findings from your project
- Performance metrics (if applicable)
- Security analysis

## Slide 12: Security Implications
- Vulnerabilities discovered or addressed
- Threat model considerations
- Potential impact in real-world scenarios

## Slide 13: Lessons Learned
- Technical insights gained
- Challenges and how they were overcome
- What you would do differently

## Slide 14: Future Work
- Potential improvements or extensions
- Unresolved issues
- Research directions

## Slide 15: Conclusion
- Summary of contributions
- Key takeaways
- Final thoughts

## Slide 16: Q&A
- Thank you slide
- Contact information
- References (if needed)

## Presentation Tips:
1. Keep text minimal - use bullet points, not paragraphs
2. Include relevant visuals (diagrams, screenshots, etc.)
3. Ensure all text is readable (font size 18pt minimum)
4. Practice timing to stay within the 10-12 minute limit
5. Prepare for potential questions
6. Both team members should present portions of the project
7. Highlight the security aspects throughout the presentation
