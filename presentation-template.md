## 🎯 Presentation Tips

### 📝 Content Guidelines:
- **Be specific:** Use concrete examples and quantitative results
- **Stay focused:** Each slide should have one main point
- **Tell a story:** Create a logical flow from problem to solution
- **Show, don't just tell:** Use visuals, diagrams, and demonstrations

### 🎨 Design Best Practices:
- **Consistent formatting:** Use the same fonts, colors, and layout
- **Readable text:** Minimum 24pt font for body text, 32pt for titles
- **High contrast:** Dark text on light backgrounds
- **Minimal text:** Use bullet points, not paragraphs
- **Professional appearance:** Clean, uncluttered slides

### 🗣️ Delivery Tips:
- **Practice timing:** Rehearse to stay within 10-12 minutes
- **Prepare for questions:** Anticipate potential questions
- **Test technology:** Ensure your demo works in the presentation environment
- **Engage audience:** Make eye contact, speak clearly
- **Handle nerves:** Practice deep breathing, arrive early

### 🔧 Technical Considerations:
- **Backup plans:** Have screenshots if live demo fails
- **File formats:** Save as both .pptx and .pdf
- **Compatibility:** Test on presentation computer beforehand
- **Remote access:** Have cloud backup of presentation

## ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Too much text:** Slides should support your speech, not replace it
- **Reading slides:** Engage with audience, don't just read
- **Technical jargon:** Explain concepts clearly for mixed audience
- **Rushing:** Speak at moderate pace, pause for emphasis
- **Ignoring time:** Practice with timer, have contingency plan
- **Demo failures:** Always have backup screenshots/videos

## 📊 Evaluation Criteria

Your presentation will be evaluated on:
- **Content quality (40%):** Technical depth, accuracy, completeness
- **Organization (20%):** Logical flow, clear structure
- **Delivery (20%):** Speaking skills, engagement, timing
- **Visual aids (10%):** Slide design, readability, effectiveness
- **Demonstration (10%):** Technical execution, explanation

---

*Remember: Your presentation should demonstrate both technical competence and communication skills. Practice multiple times and be prepared to adapt if technical issues arise.*
