Problem 2: Greatest Common Divisor

Find GCD(48, 18) using Euclidean algorithm

Step 1: 48 = 18 × 2 + 12

Step 2: 18 = 12 × 1 + 6

Step 3: 12 = 6 × 2 + 0

Therefore, GCD(48, 18) = 6

Problem 3: Modular Inverse

Find the modular inverse of 7 mod 26

Using Extended Euclidean Algorithm:

7 × 15 = 105

105 mod 26 = 1

Therefore, 7⁻¹ ≡ 15 (mod 26)

Problem 4: Modular Exponentiation

Calculate: 3⁵ mod 7

Step 1: 3⁵ = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 243

Step 2: 243 ÷ 7 = 34 remainder 5

Step 3: Therefore, 3⁵ mod 7 = 5

Problem 5: RSA Encryption

Given: n = 77, e = 7, M = 5
Calculate: C = M^e mod n

Step 1: C = 5⁷ mod 77

Step 2: 5⁷ = 78125

Step 3: 78125 ÷ 77 = 1014 remainder 47

Step 4: Therefore, C = 47
