## AI Usage Guidelines and Ethics

### Recommended AI Prompts for Learning Enhancement

#### For Concept Clarification:
```
"I'm learning about [security concept]. Can you explain [specific aspect] in simpler terms and provide a real-world analogy?"
```

#### For Example Generation:
```
"Can you provide 3 additional examples of how [security principle] applies in modern technology, different from [examples I already know]?"
```

#### For Analysis Structure:
```
"I need to analyze a security incident involving [brief description]. What framework or approach would help me structure this analysis comprehensively?"
```

#### For Understanding Verification:
```
"I believe [security concept] works like this: [your explanation]. Is my understanding correct? What am I missing?"
```

### Academic Integrity Statement
- **Always cite when AI helps generate ideas or explanations**
- **Use AI to enhance, not replace, your critical thinking**
- **Verify AI-provided information with authoritative sources**
- **Your final analysis and conclusions must be your own work**
